Foundations of Trustworthy AI

Three components that should hold throughout an AI system’s entire life cycle.

Lawful01

Respects all applicable laws and regulations

  • Applies across the whole life cycle
  • Ethics adds to legal compliance, not replaces it
  • Guidelines focus on the other two pillars
  • Guidelines are voluntary, not binding
Legal detail sits in law, not the guidelines
Ethical02

Respects ethical principles and values

  • Respect for human autonomy
  • Prevention of harm
  • Fairness
  • Explicability
Four ethical principles of the HLEG
Robust03

Sound technically and in its social setting

  • Technical robustness and safety
  • Social environment is considered
  • Good intentions can still cause harm
  • Testing and validating
Technical and social perspective
Why all three? Each is necessary but not sufficient. Ideally all three work in harmony.
Source: EU High-Level Expert Group on AI, Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI (8 April 2019). digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu
